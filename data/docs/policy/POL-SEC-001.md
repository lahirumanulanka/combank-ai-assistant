# Information Security Policy

**Policy ID:** POL-SEC-001 · **Owner:** Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) · **Version:** 3.1 · **Review:** yearly

## 1. Purpose
This policy sets the minimum security rules that protect Commercial Bank's systems, customer data and staff.
It applies to all employees, contractors and vendors who use bank systems.

## 2. Scope
All internal systems, cloud services, laptops, mobile devices and network equipment owned or managed by the bank.

## 3. Data classification
- **Public:** approved for customers and the public (for example the customer FAQ).
- **Internal:** for all staff; not for customers.
- **Confidential:** limited to teams that need it (for example architecture of identity systems).
- **Restricted:** the most sensitive data (salaries, secrets, HSM access). Named people only.

## 4. Authentication
4.1 Passwords for all internal systems must be rotated every 90 days and must not reuse the previous 12 passwords.
Minimum length is 14 characters.

4.2 Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for remote access and for all privileged accounts.

4.3 Exceptions to 4.1 and 4.2 require written approval from the Chief Information Security Officer and expire
after 6 months. Expired exceptions are disabled automatically.

## 5. Access control
5.1 Access is granted on a least-privilege basis and reviewed every quarter by the line manager.
5.2 Leavers' accounts are disabled within 4 hours of their last working day.

## 6. Incident reporting
Suspected security incidents must be reported to the Security Operations Centre within 1 hour of discovery.
See RB-OPS-001 for the incident command process.

## 7. Compliance
Breaches of this policy may lead to disciplinary action.
