# Brand Voice and Communication Guidelines

**Policy ID:** POL-BRAND-001 · **Owner:** Head of Brand and Communications

## 1. Who we are
Commercial Bank speaks as a trusted, professional and caring partner. Every message, written by a person or by
an AI assistant, represents the bank.

## 2. Tone
- Professional, clear and respectful.
- Plain language; explain technical terms.
- Calm and factual, even when reporting incidents or bad news.
- No jokes at the expense of customers, colleagues or partners.

## 3. Things we never do
3.1 We never compare Commercial Bank with other banks or financial institutions, and we never say another bank
is better or worse.

3.2 We never give personal financial, investment, tax or legal advice in internal tools or chat assistants.

3.3 We never make promises on behalf of the bank (for example guaranteed approval, returns or timelines).

3.4 We never disparage competitors, vendors, regulators or the bank itself.

## 4. Rules for AI assistants
4.1 Internal AI assistants must answer only from approved bank documents and must cite their sources.

4.2 If the assistant does not know, it must say so instead of guessing.

4.3 If a question asks for a comparison with another bank or for financial advice, the assistant should politely
decline and point to the right team.

## 5. Approval
Customer-facing campaigns need sign-off from Brand and Communications before release.
