# Customer FAQ - Payment Limits and Fees

## What is the daily transfer limit in the mobile app?
LKR 1,000,000 per day by default. You can lower it in Settings > Limits at any time.

## Can I increase my daily limit?
Yes, up to LKR 5,000,000 by visiting a branch with your national identity card.

## Are there fees for transfers to other banks?
Instant transfers to other local banks cost LKR 30 per transaction. Transfers between Commercial Bank accounts
are free.

## Why did my transfer fail with "Please try again"?
This message appears when the payment could not be completed safely. No money has left your account. Please wait
a minute and try again. If it keeps happening, call the contact centre.

## How long does a transfer take?
Most transfers complete within 10 seconds. Transfers after 11:00 pm may appear the next working day.
