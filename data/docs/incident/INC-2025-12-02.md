# Partner gateway TLS handshake failures (PayHub)

**Incident ID:** INC-2025-12-02 · **Severity:** SEV3 · **Service:** payment-gateway · **Date:** 2025-12-02 · **Duration:** 95 minutes

## Timeline
- 08:30 - Error rate jumps; logs show `SSL handshake failed`.
- 08:34 - Alert fires; on-call engineer acknowledges.
- 08:37 - Incident declared SEV3.
- 09:33 - Requested an emergency certificate from PayHub.
- 09:33 - Installed the new certificate.
- 10:05 - Incident resolved; monitoring continued for 2 hours.

## Impact
Bill payments through PayHub failed for 95 minutes; about 1,200 payments were delayed.

## Root cause
The client certificate for the PayHub partner connection expired. Certificates are renewed by hand using the Cert Register spreadsheet and there is no automated expiry alert, so nobody noticed in time. Connections failed with `SSL handshake failed: certificate has expired` (see RB-SEC-001 "Known gaps").

## Resolution
Requested an emergency certificate from PayHub. Installed the new certificate.

## Follow-up actions
1. Automated certificate rotation with 30-day expiry alerts (owner: information-security).
2. Weekly scan for certificates that expire within 45 days.
