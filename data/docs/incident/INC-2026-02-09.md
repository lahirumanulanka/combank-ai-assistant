# Elevated declines after release 4.12

**Incident ID:** INC-2026-02-09 · **Severity:** SEV3 · **Service:** payment-gateway · **Date:** 2026-02-09 · **Duration:** 140 minutes

## Timeline
- 10:05 - Decline rate rises shortly after a deployment.
- 10:09 - Alert fires; on-call engineer acknowledges.
- 10:12 - Incident declared SEV3.
- 11:38 - Turned off `strict_cvv` in production.
- 11:38 - Compared staging and production flags.
- 12:25 - Incident resolved; monitoring continued for 2 hours.

## Impact
Card-not-present payments were declined three times more often than normal for over 2 hours.

## Root cause
Release 4.12 turned on the `strict_cvv` feature flag in production only. Staging and production configuration had drifted apart because environment settings are edited by hand, and the difference was not caught before release.

## Resolution
Turned off `strict_cvv` in production. Compared staging and production flags.

## Follow-up actions
1. Keep environment configuration as code in git.
2. Add an automated drift check between staging and production.
