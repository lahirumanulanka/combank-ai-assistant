# Payment gateway timeouts during salary-day peak

**Incident ID:** INC-2026-03-14 · **Severity:** SEV2 · **Service:** payment-gateway · **Date:** 2026-03-14

## Summary
Between 17:05 and 18:20 IST on 14 March 2026, about 18% of API payments timed out. Salary-day traffic reached
2.4x the weekday baseline. 2,340 transactions failed and were retried by customers; no duplicate postings happened.

## Timeline
- 17:05 - p99 latency on `POST /payments` rises above 8 s.
- 17:09 - Alert `payment-gateway-p99-high` fires; on-call SRE acknowledges.
- 17:12 - Incident declared SEV2.
- 17:25 - Core banking adapter logs show repeated `ERR_CONN_POOL_EXHAUSTED`; pool at 64/64.
- 17:40 - Adapter replicas scaled from 4 to 8; reconciliation batch paused.
- 18:20 - Incident resolved.

## Impact
Mobile app showed "Please try again"; the contact centre received 212 calls. No financial loss.

## Root cause
The core banking adapter uses a fixed pool of 64 connections shared by online payments and the reconciliation
batch. Under salary-day load the batch held 40+ connections, so online requests queued until they timed out.
There is no isolation between batch and online work (see ARCH-CORE-001 "Known risks").

## Resolution
Scaling adapter replicas doubled pool capacity; pausing the batch released connections at once.

## Follow-up actions
1. Dynamic pool sizing with a floor for online traffic (owner: platform team, due 2026-04-30).
2. Move the reconciliation batch to a dedicated adapter instance (owner: payments-platform).
3. Load test at 3x baseline before the next salary day.
