# Payment gateway timeouts during salary-day peak

**Incident ID:** INC-2026-03-14 · **Severity:** SEV2 · **Service:** payment-gateway · **Date:** 2026-03-14 · **Duration:** 75 minutes

## Timeline
- 17:05 - p99 latency rises above 8 s and timeouts begin.
- 17:09 - Alert fires; on-call engineer acknowledges.
- 17:12 - Incident declared SEV2.
- 17:55 - Scaled adapter replicas from 4 to 8.
- 17:55 - Paused the reconciliation batch.
- 18:20 - Incident resolved; monitoring continued for 2 hours.

## Impact
About 18% of API payments timed out; 2,340 transactions failed and the contact centre received 212 calls. No financial loss.

## Root cause
Salary-day traffic at 2.4x baseline while the reconciliation batch held 40+ connections pushed the core banking adapter's fixed pool of 64 connections to 64/64. Online payment requests queued until they timed out and the adapter logged `ERR_CONN_POOL_EXHAUSTED`. Batch jobs and online traffic share the same pool with no isolation (see ARCH-CORE-001 "Known risks").

## Resolution
Scaled adapter replicas from 4 to 8. Paused the reconciliation batch.

## Follow-up actions
1. Dynamic pool sizing with a guaranteed floor for online traffic (owner: platform-engineering).
2. Run batch jobs on a dedicated adapter instance.
3. Load test at 3x baseline before peak days.
