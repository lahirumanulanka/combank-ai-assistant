# Internal mTLS certificate expiry - API payments down 42 minutes

**Incident ID:** INC-2026-07-21 · **Severity:** SEV1 · **Service:** payment-gateway · **Date:** 2026-07-21

## Summary
At 09:14 IST on 21 July 2026 all API payments failed for 42 minutes. The internal mTLS certificate used between
payment-gateway and the core banking adapter expired at midnight UTC and nobody was alerted.

## Timeline
- 09:14 - Error rate on `POST /payments` jumps to 100%.
- 09:16 - Alert fires; on-call SRE sees `SSL handshake failed: certificate has expired`.
- 09:20 - Incident declared SEV1; incident commander assigned.
- 09:41 - Emergency certificate issued from the internal CA.
- 09:56 - New certificate deployed; payments recover.

## Impact
About 31,000 payment attempts failed. Partner banks were informed. No money was lost; all attempts were rejected
before posting.

## Root cause
The certificate was renewed by hand using the Cert Register spreadsheet, and this certificate was missing from the
register. There is no automated expiry alerting (see RB-SEC-001 "Known gaps").

## Resolution
Emergency certificate issued and deployed.

## Follow-up actions
1. Automated certificate rotation with 30-day expiry alerts (owner: information-security).
2. Scan the service mesh weekly for certificates expiring within 45 days.
3. Post-incident review held in MTG-2026-07-PIR.
