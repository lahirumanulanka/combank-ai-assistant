# Card authorization failures - September month-end

**Incident ID:** INC-2025-09-30 · **Severity:** SEV2 · **Service:** card-switch · **Date:** 2025-09-30 · **Duration:** 65 minutes

## Timeline
- 19:40 - p99 latency rises above 8 s and timeouts begin.
- 19:44 - Alert fires; on-call engineer acknowledges.
- 19:47 - Incident declared SEV2.
- 20:23 - Paused the settlement batch.
- 20:23 - Added 2 adapter replicas.
- 20:45 - Incident resolved; monitoring continued for 2 hours.

## Impact
About 9% of card authorizations were declined for 65 minutes; 4,100 card payments failed.

## Root cause
The month-end settlement batch pushed the core banking adapter's fixed pool of 64 connections to 64/64. Online payment requests queued until they timed out and the adapter logged `ERR_CONN_POOL_EXHAUSTED`. Batch jobs and online traffic share the same pool with no isolation (see ARCH-CORE-001 "Known risks").

## Resolution
Paused the settlement batch. Added 2 adapter replicas.

## Follow-up actions
1. Dynamic pool sizing with a guaranteed floor for online traffic (owner: platform-engineering).
2. Run batch jobs on a dedicated adapter instance.
3. Load test at 3x baseline before peak days.
