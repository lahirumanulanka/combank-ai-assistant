# Card authorization timeouts during evening peak

**Incident ID:** INC-2025-11-18 · **Severity:** SEV2 · **Service:** card-switch · **Date:** 2025-11-18 · **Duration:** 50 minutes

## Timeline
- 20:10 - p99 latency rises above 8 s and timeouts begin.
- 20:14 - Alert fires; on-call engineer acknowledges.
- 20:17 - Incident declared SEV2.
- 20:43 - Stopped the delayed batch.
- 20:43 - Scaled adapter replicas from 4 to 6.
- 21:00 - Incident resolved; monitoring continued for 2 hours.

## Impact
Card authorizations slowed to 9 seconds; merchants saw about 2,800 declines.

## Root cause
The evening shopping peak together with a delayed nightly batch pushed the core banking adapter's fixed pool of 64 connections to 64/64. Online payment requests queued until they timed out and the adapter logged `ERR_CONN_POOL_EXHAUSTED`. Batch jobs and online traffic share the same pool with no isolation (see ARCH-CORE-001 "Known risks").

## Resolution
Stopped the delayed batch. Scaled adapter replicas from 4 to 6.

## Follow-up actions
1. Dynamic pool sizing with a guaranteed floor for online traffic (owner: platform-engineering).
2. Run batch jobs on a dedicated adapter instance.
3. Load test at 3x baseline before peak days.
