# Card scheme connection certificate expiry

**Incident ID:** INC-2026-04-03 · **Severity:** SEV2 · **Service:** card-switch · **Date:** 2026-04-03 · **Duration:** 55 minutes

## Timeline
- 00:05 - Error rate jumps; logs show `SSL handshake failed`.
- 00:09 - Alert fires; on-call engineer acknowledges.
- 00:12 - Incident declared SEV2.
- 00:41 - Moved traffic to the second scheme link.
- 00:41 - Installed a renewed certificate.
- 01:00 - Incident resolved; monitoring continued for 2 hours.

## Impact
Half of international card authorizations failed for 55 minutes.

## Root cause
The mTLS certificate for one of the two card scheme links expired. Certificates are renewed by hand using the Cert Register spreadsheet and there is no automated expiry alert, so nobody noticed in time. Connections failed with `SSL handshake failed: certificate has expired` (see RB-SEC-001 "Known gaps").

## Resolution
Moved traffic to the second scheme link. Installed a renewed certificate.

## Follow-up actions
1. Automated certificate rotation with 30-day expiry alerts (owner: information-security).
2. Weekly scan for certificates that expire within 45 days.
