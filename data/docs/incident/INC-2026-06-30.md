# Quarter-end batch starves online payment connections

**Incident ID:** INC-2026-06-30 · **Severity:** SEV2 · **Service:** core-banking-adapter · **Date:** 2026-06-30 · **Duration:** 60 minutes

## Timeline
- 21:15 - p99 latency rises above 8 s and timeouts begin.
- 21:19 - Alert fires; on-call engineer acknowledges.
- 21:22 - Incident declared SEV2.
- 21:55 - Throttled the interest batch.
- 21:55 - Added 3 adapter replicas.
- 22:15 - Incident resolved; monitoring continued for 2 hours.

## Impact
Online payments failed or slowed for an hour; about 3,600 transactions failed.

## Root cause
The quarter-end interest batch, which opened 50 connections, pushed the core banking adapter's fixed pool of 64 connections to 64/64. Online payment requests queued until they timed out and the adapter logged `ERR_CONN_POOL_EXHAUSTED`. Batch jobs and online traffic share the same pool with no isolation (see ARCH-CORE-001 "Known risks").

## Resolution
Throttled the interest batch. Added 3 adapter replicas.

## Follow-up actions
1. Dynamic pool sizing with a guaranteed floor for online traffic (owner: platform-engineering).
2. Run batch jobs on a dedicated adapter instance.
3. Load test at 3x baseline before peak days.
