# Internet banking login outage - IdP signing key

**Incident ID:** INC-2026-02-27 · **Severity:** SEV1 · **Service:** identity · **Date:** 2026-02-27 · **Duration:** 70 minutes

## Timeline
- 07:45 - Login failures spike on internet banking and the mobile app.
- 07:49 - Alert fires; on-call engineer acknowledges.
- 07:52 - Incident declared SEV1.
- 08:31 - Rolled back to the previous signing key.
- 08:31 - Re-published the new key.
- 08:55 - Incident resolved; monitoring continued for 2 hours.

## Impact
Customers could not log in to internet banking or the mobile app for 70 minutes.

## Root cause
A planned signing key rotation skipped the 7-day pre-publish step. The new signing key became active before all apps had downloaded it, so tokens were rejected with `invalid_signature` (see ARCH-IAM-001).

## Resolution
Rolled back to the previous signing key. Re-published the new key.

## Follow-up actions
1. Publish new signing keys 7 days before they become active.
2. Automate the rotation steps in RB-IAM-001.
