# Christmas Eve payment failures - gateway unresponsive

**Incident ID:** INC-2025-12-24 · **Severity:** SEV1 · **Service:** payment-gateway · **Date:** 2025-12-24 · **Duration:** 110 minutes

## Timeline
- 18:00 - p99 latency rises above 8 s and timeouts begin.
- 18:04 - Alert fires; on-call engineer acknowledges.
- 18:07 - Incident declared SEV1.
- 19:13 - Paused the reconciliation batch.
- 19:13 - Scaled adapter replicas from 4 to 10.
- 19:13 - Restarted stuck gateway pods.
- 19:50 - Incident resolved; monitoring continued for 2 hours.

## Impact
About 35% of payments failed for almost 2 hours; 14,900 transactions failed and many customers complained on social media. Post-incident review: MTG-2026-02-PIR.

## Root cause
Christmas Eve traffic at 3.1x baseline together with the year-end reconciliation batch pushed the core banking adapter's fixed pool of 64 connections to 64/64. Online payment requests queued until they timed out and the adapter logged `ERR_CONN_POOL_EXHAUSTED`. Batch jobs and online traffic share the same pool with no isolation (see ARCH-CORE-001 "Known risks").

## Resolution
Paused the reconciliation batch. Scaled adapter replicas from 4 to 10. Restarted stuck gateway pods.

## Follow-up actions
1. Dynamic pool sizing with a guaranteed floor for online traffic (owner: platform-engineering).
2. Run batch jobs on a dedicated adapter instance.
3. Load test at 3x baseline before peak days.
